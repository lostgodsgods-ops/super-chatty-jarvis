About the project

The long-term vision is a mobile-friendly control center that can connect approved AI services, organize tasks, search trusted knowledge sources, and support future robotics experiments. Features shown here describe the project vision; they are not claims that a live platform or autonomous agents are already running.

🧠

AI assistant & agents

Explore an orchestrator that routes tasks to approved AI providers and keeps a clear activity record.

🦾

Robotics knowledge

Organize learning materials on ROS, sensors, motors, simulation, computer vision, and safe control.

🛡️

Safety & owner control

Design around explicit permissions, human approval for sensitive actions, auditability, and an emergency stop.

Planned capabilities

Possible modules, subject to implementation, testing, and appropriate credentials.

🌐 Knowledge hub

Curated public references, source links, document dates, and searchable notes.

🔌 AI integrations

Optional API-based connections to AI providers. API keys must be stored securely server-side, never in public browser code.

📱 Mobile control panel

A responsive website/PWA concept for projects, agent status, logs, and owner-approved actions.
